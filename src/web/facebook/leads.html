Create a test form in Meta
  1. Open Meta Business Suite and switch to the same Page you connected here.
  2. Open All tools → Instant Forms → Create form. Add name, email and phone questions and your business privacy policy, then publish the form.
  3. Reload forms here, enable syncing for the published form, and open Meta Lead Ads Testing Tool to submit a test lead.

In Meta App Dashboard, the Capture & manage ad leads use case is the relevant setup for retrieving leads. An advertising sandbox account is for testing ads; choose your actual test Page when creating an Instant Form.
