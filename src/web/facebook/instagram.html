Meta App Review recording guide

For profile access, sign in with the supplied application login, then follow these steps using your own Instagram professional account. Enter Instagram credentials only on Instagram's authorization screen.

  1. Click Connect with Instagram, sign into your professional account, and grant access. Select the authorized Instagram account to finish connecting.
  2. Under Connected Instagram profile, show the username and Instagram account ID. Click Refresh profile to retrieve current profile information, then View profile on Instagram to compare the account. You can return to this section using Open account on its card.
  3. From another Instagram account, send a DM to the connected account. Refresh inbox, open the conversation, read the message and send a reply. Show the reply arriving in Instagram.
  4. Comment on a post from the other account. Refresh posts, select the post, refresh comments, then reply to and hide/unhide the comment. Show the result on Instagram.
  5. For publishing access, reconnect with Instagram, upload a JPEG or MP4, enter a caption, prepare the post and publish it. Show the resulting post on Instagram.

Direct Instagram login uses instagram_business_manage_messages for DMs, instagram_business_manage_comments for comments and instagram_business_basic for profile and posts. The Facebook connection remains available for professional accounts linked to a Page. Publishing photos and Reels uses instagram_business_content_publish.

instagram_business_basic identifies the connected profile and lists its media. It is also requested as a dependent permission for instagram_business_manage_messages and instagram_business_manage_comments.
